# Local Browser Dependency

`lucide.min.js` is the unmodified UMD distribution of the locally installed
Lucide 1.8.0 package. Its ISC license and notices for Feather-derived icons
are preserved in `LUCIDE-LICENSE`. Upstream: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide.
No CDN or external font/icon request is required by the workbench.
